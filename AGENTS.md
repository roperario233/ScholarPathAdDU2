# ScholarPath AdDU Agents Guide


## Purpose
This repository contains ScholarPath AdDU, a React + Vite scholarship discovery, eligibility matching, Document Vault, application tracking, notification, calendar, and admin review prototype for Ateneo de Davao University.


This file is the operating guide for any future coding agent working in this workspace.


## Source Of Truth Priority
When deciding what to change, use this priority order:


1. The manuscript and its stated research objectives, scope, limitations, terminology, and evaluation design.
2. The actual codebase and its current working behavior.
3. Future enhancements or refactors that are not already supported by the manuscript or the current app.


If the manuscript and the codebase conflict, preserve the manuscript intent first and then make the smallest code change that keeps the app stable.


## Product Context
ScholarPath AdDU is a centralized hybrid web and mobile system for student scholarship discovery and application tracking. The manuscript defines the core system goals as:


- Centralized scholarship discovery across AdDU's financial aid pipelines.
- A Smart Eligibility Checker that evaluates QPI, household income, degree program, and exclusion rules.
- A Document Vault that supports one-time upload and multi-application reuse.
- A notification subsystem for deadline and status alerts.
- Role-based access control for students, Admissions Office Staff and the Admissions Office Head, and Department Chairs.
- A student application workspace for filtering, submitting, inspecting, and exporting application reports.
- An Admissions Office workspace for maintaining AdDU internal scholarship catalog entries and coordinating central application review.
- A reusable Document Vault with upload validation, verification states, and application links.
- A deadline calendar supporting scholarship deadlines and student-created reminders.
- Configurable notification channels and reminder timing, with an in-app notification center.
- A polished task-based usability prototype evaluated with ISO/IEC 25010 and SUS.


The manuscript also establishes important scope boundaries that must stay intact:


- The system is discovery, matching, upload, and tracking focused.
- It does not implement actual financial disbursement.
- It does not integrate with the registrar for verified QPI.
- It does not give external scholarship organizations direct administrative access.
- It remains a prototype. Supabase is required for data and authentication; the demo mode, its seeded data, and the fallback login were removed, which reverses the earlier scope statement that the system "must continue to work in demo mode when Supabase is not configured."


## Canonical Manuscript Terms
Use the manuscript language consistently in new code, UI text, documentation, and comments where relevant:


- Smart Eligibility Checker.
- Exclusion Flag Hierarchy.
- Document Vault.
- Dynamic Faceted Search.
- Admissions Office Staff and Admissions Office Head (the manuscript's Admissions Office administrators, split into two accountability lanes).
- Department Chairs and Coordinators.
- Grant-in-Aid or GIA.
- Financial aid pipelines.
- Application tracking.
- Application workspace.
- Notification center.
- Deadline calendar.

The Admissions Office is one central office with two role values: `admissions_office` is **Admissions Office Staff** and `admissions_head` is the **Admissions Office Head**. Neither is a Dean or a reviewer for every academic department. Staff prepare a case — intake, document verification, the hand-off to endorsement, interview scheduling, and the sub-committee recommendation. The Head is a **superset**: they can perform every Staff stage and also own endorsement (SOP step 4), approval (SOP step 7), results release (SOP step 8), the catalog, and announcements. Department Chair access is read-only and scoped to the Chair's assigned department: the Chair reads the department's cases, endorsement records, and interviews and may record panel attendance and evaluation notes, but no stage move (endorsement included) belongs to the Chair. SOP step 5a draws the interview panel from the school where the applicant's program belongs, so a Department Chair may sit on an interview panel and record its outcome. The Admissions Office still coordinates the interview and owns every status move. OSA is not a participating scholarship-process role in the web app.


Avoid introducing alternate names for the same feature unless the current codebase already uses a different stable label that users see.


## Current Codebase Map
The present implementation is organized as follows:


- `src/App.jsx` is the main state and routing-style coordinator for authentication, app state, theme, profile hydration and saving (`saveProfile`), notification generation, deadline reminders, and view switching. It persists the app state in localStorage under `scholarpath-addu-state` (with a one-time migration from the legacy `scholarpath-addu-demo-state` key).
- `src/pages/` contains the main screens:
  - `LoginScreen.jsx` — Google OAuth accepts personal Google accounts as well as AdDU accounts; do not send a Google hosted-domain (`hd`) restriction. Account creation and password-reset forms accept a general email address.
  - `DashboardView.jsx` — the student Dashboard only (welcome hero, next best action, deadline calendar, top matches, notifications/announcements). Staff, head, and Department Chair roles have no separate Dashboard route: both Admissions Office lanes land on the office Dashboard (`admin`), and a Department Chair lands on `review` (Department Review).
  - `ScholarshipExplorer.jsx` — scholarship discovery with the inline Smart Eligibility Checker on each scholarship card.
  - `ApplicationsView.jsx` — student application filtering, progress, detail modal (requirement checklist, supporting documents, statement, profile snapshot, timeline), and text report export (which includes the supporting documents, each rejection reason, and the full profile snapshot). While an application is `Submitted`, `Under Review`, or `For Verification`, a `Rejected` attached file shows its reason with a `RejectedDocumentReplacer` (pick another vault file or upload a correction into the vault first), and the application card asks for the replacement. A Draft opens the application workspace (`src/components/ApplicationWorkspaceModal.jsx`), a four-step flow: attach each required document from the Document Vault (or upload a new file, which lands in the vault first, or, for a photo requirement, a camera capture) and, optionally, other vault files as supporting documents, review the self-reported profile snapshot (everything it lists is sent), write the statement (prefilled from the default scholarship statement in My Profile; the student can reset to it or save the tailored text back as the new default), then certify and submit. External and government-linked entries are tracked only: no checklist and no submit.
  - `DocumentVaultView.jsx` — student document upload, search/filter, verification display, and deletion. A `Rejected` file shows its reason and, when an in-scope application before endorsement still uses it, an action that opens that application's details to replace it. The upload form is attribute-driven: one grouped picker (mirroring the program/course picker, `buildDocumentPickerOptions(profile)`) lists each profile attribute with the document types it accepts for the student's standing below it, plus a leading "General documents" group for types that prove no answer; a type that proves an answer is listed only under its attribute (with "also proves …" when it proves more than one), picking an item chooses the attribute and document type together, and the vault derives the title from the pair, so there is no free-text title and no attribute checklist. The hint under the picker names every answer the upload will prove. The two photo types (`Applicant Photo`, `Photos of Residence`) offer a camera or an existing photo, show the shared `PhotoGuide`, and hold the captured file in form state so a photo needs no file-picker round trip.
  - `src/pages/admin/` — the Admissions Office workspace, split into one route per function instead of a single monolithic console: `AdminOverview.jsx` (`admin`, the **Dashboard** landing route for both office lanes, with the review/document/release metrics, the links into each workspace, and the recent application activity feed derived from status logs and timelines), `AdminReviewQueue.jsx` (`admin-review`, the review queue and SOP stage track; opening a Submitted application starts its review, each entry's primary action is Start review / Open review / Review documents, and an entry only badges documents still to verify once its status unlocks them), `AdminInterviews.jsx` (`admin-interviews`, the SOP step-5 operations board: endorsed applicants awaiting a schedule, upcoming interviews grouped by Manila date with attendance actions, and roster-level panelist/room clashes; scheduling and rescheduling still open the shared review modal), `AdminCalendar.jsx` (`admin-calendar`, the office month view of scheduled interviews against the in-scope catalog deadlines, with a day agenda; the Head's venue manager lives here), `AdminScholarshipCatalog.jsx` (`admin-catalog`, AdDU internal scholarship publishing/editing and the required-documents editor; leaving the route with unsaved input asks before discarding), `AdminResultsRelease.jsx` (`admin-release`, the Head's approval and release lane plus the accepted-list export), and `AdminAnnouncements.jsx` (`admin-announcements`, announcement publishing and the published list). Their derivations live in `src/lib/admissionsOffice.js`, and every page shares `src/components/AdminPageHeader.jsx` so the workspace keeps one title surface per page. Document Vault verification happens inside each application's `ApplicationReviewModal`, the shared staff review surface: a fixed header (applicant, status, `SopStageTrack`, documents-verified count), Overview / Profile snapshot / Documents / Endorsement / Interview / History tabs (Profile snapshot sits beside Overview, left of Documents), and one sticky action bar that renders one button per transition from `getNextApplicationStatuses(status, role)` apart from the automatic `AUTOMATIC_STAGE_STATUSES` moves, labelled by `getStageActionLabel()` (the sub-committee note field sits in that bar beside its action; the interview schedule is filled in the Interview tab and confirmed by the bar's **Schedule interview** button). Its Documents tab is hidden until the application reaches `For Verification` (stage gate `areDocumentsReviewable()`, visibility `areApplicationDocumentsVisible()` which keeps a Rejected application's files readable), and the footer explains the SOP step-3 gate instead, beside the `Continue to verification` action that opens the file queue in place; once unlocked it lists the required submissions and supporting files on the left and, from the `lg` breakpoint, previews the selected file inline (`DocumentPreview` from `DocumentPreviewModal.jsx`) beside its `DocumentVerificationItem` details (what the file proves, the value declared at upload, and the outdated state); the verification actions and the file's "Open in a new tab" link sit together at the top of the pane, above the preview. From `lg` the requirement list and the verification pane scroll independently, so verifying one file never moves the other; below `lg` they stack in the modal body's single scroll and the preview opens in its own modal. The Verified/Rejected actions are available to both Admissions Office lanes, and Department Chairs see the same pane read-only. The Endorsement tab appears from `For Endorsement` on (stage gate `areEndorsementRecordsVisible()`), once the Admissions Office has handed the case over — it stays hidden while the case is still with the office for verification — and shows the endorsement record (the hand-off and the recorded decision), one internal comment thread, and the attached-document readiness line. The stage action itself stays the sticky action bar's single transition button (Send to endorsement for the Admissions Office, Endorse for the Head), so the action is never rendered twice while the tab is open. The Interview tab (SOP step 5, `src/components/InterviewPanel.jsx`) appears from `Endorsed` on for the Admissions Office and Department Chairs when the page passes `interviewWorkspace`. There is no standalone document list, so a Pending file that no queued application relies on is not reviewed until the student submits it with an application.
  - `DepartmentReviewView.jsx` — department-scoped, read-only Chair review workspace; the Chair opens the review modal's Endorsement tab to read the hand-off and the Office of Admissions & Aid decision on applications that have reached endorsement, and takes no stage action of its own. At `Interview` a row opens the Interview tab ("Open interview" when the Chair sits on the panel, "View interview" otherwise), and the "Interviews on your panel" stat counts the Chair's panel assignments. Endorsement remains `For Endorsement` → `Endorsed`, and only when every attached file is Verified.
  - `DepartmentCalendarView.jsx` (`chair-calendar`) and `DepartmentInterviewsView.jsx` (`chair-interviews`) — the Chair's own SOP step-5 surfaces over the department queue. The calendar marks interviews the Chair sits on apart from the rest of the department, plus the in-scope catalog deadlines; the interviews page lists panel work with inline attendance and a link into the review modal's Interview tab for evaluation notes. Neither page offers scheduling, rescheduling, or cancellation: the Admissions Office coordinates SOP step 5. A Chair who is not on a panel sees the department interview **View only**, matching the RLS and the modal's existing read-only lane. Venue text comes from the saved `interview.locationLabel` because `interview_locations` is not loaded for a Chair session.
  - `CalendarView.jsx`

Address, Family details, Academic profile, Household and financial aid, and Eligibility background are student-only. Personal information carries the profile photo (captured or uploaded; personal to the signed-in user, never sent with an application, and shown without the application-photo guide), the short bio for every role plus a student-only default scholarship statement (stored as `essay`), an optional template that prefills each new application statement and is not counted toward profile completeness. The Overview presents the profile-completeness checklist and, for students, the student-only Documents for applications card. Its completeness checklist (`getProfileCompleteness()` in `src/lib/profile.js`) follows `getStandingRequirements()`: an item that does not apply to the student's standing is left out rather than counted as done, so an incoming first-year is asked for the senior high school general average and strand instead of an AdDU student number and QPI. ## Editing Principles
Follow these rules when making changes:


- Prefer the smallest change that solves the task.
- Reuse existing shared components, helpers, and data shapes instead of duplicating logic.
- Supabase is the required backend; do not reintroduce an offline demo mode, seeded data, or a fallback login.
- Preserve backward compatibility with stored state in localStorage, including the one-time migration from the legacy demo key.
- Fail cleanly with a clear "not configured" message when the Supabase environment variables are missing, instead of silently entering any offline mode.
- Do not introduce new dependencies unless they clearly solve the task better than the current stack.
- Do not remove or rewrite manuscript-aligned terminology just to make the code more generic.
- Avoid unnecessary refactors that change behavior, layout, or data shape.
- Treat `applications`, `documents`, `notifications`, `announcements`, `customDeadlines`, `notificationPreferences`, `profileDraft`, and `theme` as persisted state domains; add compatibility defaults when extending them.
- Keep file-upload behavior safe: the file bytes live in a private Supabase Storage bucket (`documents`), and the `documents` row records the object path in `storage_path`. Students manage only their own folder (`documents/<auth.uid()>/<documentId>/<file>`), the Admissions Office reads every document, and a Department Chair reads documents owned by students in the chair's department; viewing always goes through a short-lived signed URL. The bucket and its storage RLS policies come from `supabase/migrations/20261009000000_add_document_storage.sql`. The `protect_document_review_fields` trigger (`20261013000000_add_document_attested_values.sql`, extended by `20261021000000_replace_rejected_application_documents.sql` and `20261027000000_add_document_decision_actor.sql`) keeps the review fields staff-owned: a student's insert is forced to `Pending` with a server `uploaded_at`, and a student cannot change `verification_status`, `rejection_reason`, `verification_decided_at`, `reviewed_by`, `reviewed_by_role`, `linked_attributes`, `attested_values`, `uploaded_at`, or `owner_id` afterwards. Profile photos use a separate private `avatars` bucket (`20261026000000_add_profile_avatar.sql`) with owner-only policies and `profiles.avatar_path`; they are personal, no staff role can read them, and the app displays them through a memory-only signed URL with an initials fallback.


## Domain Rules
These rules come from the manuscript and should guide implementation details:


- Eligibility matching should honor QPI, income, degree program, active status, deadline state, and exclusion logic.
- The academic requirement branches on year standing, mirroring `getStandingRequirements()` in `src/lib/profile.js`: enrolled students (1st–5th year) are matched on the collegiate QPI, while incoming first-years are matched on their senior high school general average. The Admissions Office internal catalog stores both floors (`minimum_qpi` and `minimum_shs_average`), and `getAcademicRequirement()` in `src/lib/eligibility.js` is the single source the application gate, the general-pool evaluator, the fit score, and the requirement chips read. Honors (Jubilee) and work-study (Student Assistant) declare no academic floor, so they show no academic requirement chip.
- Specific exclusions must override broad inclusions.
- Jubilee Scholarship eligibility is not restricted to incoming first-year students; retain its official Valedictorian/Salutatorian standing and graduating-class size criteria.
- Student Assistant (SA) / Working Scholar eligibility is open to students across all academic programs and must not be blocked by degree-specific catalog metadata.
- Scholarship discovery should support faceted filtering and fast search over the current taxonomy.
- Document handling should behave like a normalized vault where the same file can be attached to multiple applications.
- Notifications should remain event-oriented; the scheduled Edge Function is the delivery path, while the in-app center reads the local and server rows.
- Role-based access should preserve student, central Admissions Office Staff and Head, and department-scoped Department Chair boundaries; never give either central office lane Dean-like scope under a department role. The two office lanes must stay distinct: Staff prepare a case and verify documents but cannot approve or release, while the Head is a superset that can do everything plus approval and release. `ADMISSIONS_OFFICE_ROLES`, `isAdmissionsStaffRole()`, `isAdmissionsHeadRole()`, and `isAdmissionsReviewerRole()` in `src/lib/constants.js` are the shared predicates.
- My Profile editing must keep the same boundaries: students edit their academic, household, and eligibility background fields, while Admissions Office administrators and Department Chairs edit only their name, mobile number, and bio. Department assignment is never self-editable because it scopes the Department Review queue; `saveProfile` in `App.jsx` enforces this with `STAFF_EDITABLE_FIELD_KEYS`, not only the UI.
- Profile QPI and household income are self-reported. They can optionally be verified by the Admissions Office against Document Vault proof, so never describe them as Registrar-verified and never imply a registrar integration.
- Year standing is the single student control (Incoming 1st year, then 1st–5th year); `applicantType` and `yearLevel` are derived from it for the eligibility engine and stored alongside it. `getStandingRequirements()` in `src/lib/profile.js` is the single source for which fields apply, and the onboarding modal, My Profile Academic profile, validators, and routing gates all read it. An incoming first-year reports ranked program choices and senior high school strand/general average instead of an AdDU student number and collegiate QPI, so profile completeness and onboarding skip those continuing-student fields. The three ranked choices are mutually exclusive: `validateAcademicSection()` trims and rejects any repeat (`2nd`/`3rd` against the 1st, `3rd` against the `2nd`) and clears an inherited ranked list when the standing no longer ranks choices, while `getProgramChoiceOptions()` in the same module powers both the `AcademicProfileModal` and My Profile `AcademicSection` pickers, hiding the programs already chosen in another slot and always keeping the slot's own current value visible so the picker never displays the wrong program. Each choice field renders its own inline error instead of a shared message.
- An application must not be submittable "whenever". Each AdDU internal catalog entry lists its required documents in `scholarships.required_documents` (`{ id, label, documentTypes, standing: 'all' | 'incoming' | 'continuing' }`), edited by the Admissions Office in the catalog form (`RequiredDocumentsEditor`) and defaulting per rule family to `DEFAULT_REQUIRED_DOCUMENTS` in `src/lib/applicationRequirements.js` (keep it identical to the backfill in `20261012000000_add_application_requirements.sql`; every in-scope family also lists `applicant-photo` (Applicant Photo) and `residence-photos` (Photos of Residence), backfilled by `20261026000001_add_applicant_photo_requirement.sql`, and `citizenship-proof` (Proof of Filipino citizenship), backfilled by `20261028000000_require_current_attribute_proofs.sql`). `getApplicationReadiness()` is the single readiness check for the workspace, the submit handler, and the staff review modal: the year standing must be set and the "Needed to apply" profile items complete, every requirement for the student's standing needs a non-rejected attached vault file (Pending is enough; verification stays at `For Verification`) that is a current proof of every answer the requirement proves (the `outdated` requirement state otherwise), every claim row needs the same, plus a statement, the certification checkbox, an open deadline, and a passing eligibility gate. A submitted application is read against its frozen snapshot and submission date (`getProofContext()`), so a later profile edit or a new academic year never flags a file the student can no longer change. The `enforce_student_application_update` trigger repeats the gate server-side (including the profile-completeness check on a Draft insert and the current-proof check on submit, `20261028000000`), limits students to creating Drafts and moving `Draft` → `Submitted`, and freezes submitted rows; `protect_submitted_application_documents` stops a student from deleting a vault file a submitted application relies on; students may change `application_documents` links only while the application is a Draft, except that a Rejected file may be replaced before endorsement (see the replace-in-place rule below).
- Application photos are captured with the device camera or chosen from the device in the application workspace, the Document Vault, and the rejected-file replacement. `src/lib/photoGuide.js` owns the framing (square `Applicant Photo`, native-aspect `Photos of Residence`), the JPEG normalization, and the bullet-point guides; `PhotoCaptureModal` opens the camera only from a tap, stops every track on close, switches between the front and rear cameras, and falls back to an existing photo or the native camera when permission is blocked or unavailable. The guide applies only to the photos submitted with an application — the profile photo deliberately has none — and the reviewer pane repeats the same criteria beside a photo file.

- Submission stores `statement`, `certified_at`, and the frozen `profile_snapshot` (`buildProfileSnapshot()`: self-reported values with their derived Document Vault verification badges, never Registrar-verified) on the application. Snapshot version 2 keeps the original fields (name, student number, program, department, year standing, QPI or senior high school general average, household income, citizenship) and adds the `eligibility` answers (academic standing, honors rank, graduating class size, the three exclusion flags, and for an incoming first-year the strand and ranked program choices), `background` (IP community, PWD, employment), `personal` (civil status, religion), `address`, and `family` groups; the default scholarship statement (`essay`) is never copied. `getProfileSnapshotSections()` is the single labelled view of a snapshot that the workspace, the student's detail modal and export, and `ApplicationReviewModal` (Admissions Office and Department Chairs alike) render; a snapshot without `version` was submitted earlier and shows only the original fields. The view leads with the applicant's 2x2 ID photo, read from the attached `Applicant Photo` Document Vault file through `getApplicantPhotoDocument()` and a short-lived signed URL, so a replacement before endorsement shows the newer file; it is never the owner-only profile photo (`profiles.avatar_path`), which is not shared with reviewers, and it is deliberately not frozen into the snapshot JSON. The text export names that file (or "Not attached") because it cannot embed an image. Students may also attach optional supporting documents: any of their own non-rejected vault files whose type matches no requirement for their standing. `getApplicationReadiness()` returns them as `supportingDocuments`; they never gate submission and use the same `application_documents` links (Draft-only, except a rejected-file replacement before endorsement). Tell the student what is shared: the workspace Profile snapshot step lists every group expanded, the Review and submit step states that the documents, statement, and full profile snapshot go to the Admissions Office and the Department Chair, and the certification checkbox doubles as the student's consent to that sharing. My Profile (section descriptions, the Religion hint, the completeness group notes) and the Document Vault upload card say the same; keep these notices aligned when the snapshot or the staff document access changes. Application ids are real uuids generated client-side and passed to `createSupabaseApplication()`, like vault documents.
- Application progress should remain status-driven (`Draft`, `Submitted`, `Under Review`, `For Verification`, `Endorsed`, `Interview`, `Recommended`, `Approved`, `Released`, and `Rejected`). Submission and each status transition must persist a trackable event in the application's `timeline`, then invoke the server-side application-status notification after the status write succeeds.
- Documents should expose verification states (`Pending`, `Verified`, and `Rejected`) and remain reusable across applications through attached document IDs. Every decision is attributable and dated: `require_document_rejection_reason` (`20261021000000`, extended by `20261027000000_add_document_decision_actor.sql`) stamps `documents.verification_decided_at`, `reviewed_by` (`auth.uid()`), and `reviewed_by_role` (`current_profile_role()`) on any status change, so a repeat rejection of the same file gets its own notification key and the current decision always names the Admissions Office account that made it. The client never sends the actor. A move back to `Pending` clears the date and the reviewer, because a reset is not a decision. Authority is unchanged: only the Admissions Office (Staff and Head) may write a decision, a Department Chair reads it, and the student sees the lane and date but never the reviewer's name. `describeDocumentDecision()` in `src/lib/applicationRequirements.js` is the single phrasing, rendered read-only by `DocumentDecisionRecord` in `src/components/ApplicationParts.jsx` in the reviewer pane, the Chair's pane, the student's Document Vault card, and the student's application details; verification itself is confirmed through `VerifyDocumentModal` before the write.
- Student recovery from a rejected Document Vault file is replace-in-place, and only before endorsement. While the application is `Submitted`, `Under Review`, or `For Verification` (`DOCUMENT_REPLACEMENT_STATUSES` in `src/lib/constants.js`), the student may swap a `Rejected` attached file for another own, non-rejected vault file — or a new upload that lands in the vault first. `replace_rejected_application_document(p_application_id, p_old_document_id, p_new_document_id)` (`20261021000000`, granted to `authenticated` only) re-checks ownership, the application status, that the old file is attached and `Rejected`, that the new file is the caller's and not already attached, and that it is the same `document_type` or shares one `required_documents[].documentTypes` entry. It deletes and inserts the `application_documents` link in one transaction and never touches the application row, so `enforce_student_application_update` is unaffected and the status stays the same. The replacement arrives `Pending`; the application may not advance until every attached file is `Verified`. The unlinked rejected file is then deletable through `protect_submitted_application_documents`, which only guards files an application still relies on. The client mirrors this with `getReplaceableRejectedDocuments()` / `getReplacementOptions()` in `src/lib/applicationRequirements.js`, `replaceRejectedDocument()` in `App.jsx`, `replaceSupabaseApplicationDocument()` in `src/lib/supabaseData.js`, and `RejectedDocumentReplacer` in `src/components/ApplicationParts.jsx`, which the student's application detail modal renders under each `Rejected` file. No `timeline` entry is written for a replacement because a student cannot write a submitted row; staff see the new `Pending` file and the queue's files-to-verify badge.
- Document Vault files are stored in the private Supabase Storage `documents` bucket; `documents.storage_path` holds the object key and `documents.id` is a real uuid generated client-side, so `application_documents.document_id` can reference it. Viewing always uses a short-lived signed URL (`getSupabaseDocumentUrl`), never a public link.
- Profile attribute verification is derived, never stored separately: a vault document proves the attribute(s) in `linkedAttributes` (client) / `documents.linked_attributes` (Supabase) plus whatever its document type proves for the owner's standing (`getProvenAttributes()` in `src/lib/profile.js`, read through `getDocumentLinkedAttributes()` in `src/lib/verification.js`), and `deriveAttributeVerifications()` maps each `VERIFIABLE_ATTRIBUTE_OPTIONS` key to `Unverified` / `Pending` / `Verified` / `Rejected` / `Outdated` / `Not applicable` / `No proof needed` / `Not answered` from those links. `getProvenAttributes()` is the single type-to-attribute map (grade report or transcript → QPI and academic standing for an enrolled student; HS report card → SHS average and academic standing for an incoming first-year; income proof or certificate of indigency → household income; certificate of award → graduation honors; PSA birth certificate, national ID, driver's license, or passport → citizenship; PWD ID or medical certificate → PWD; certificate of employment or payslip → employment; tribal certificate → IP community; Good Moral Character proves nothing). Every upload path (`uploadVaultDocument` in `App.jsx`, used by the vault, the application workspace, and the rejected-file replacement) records those links and their attested values, and `document_type_attributes()` mirrors the map in the database. An attribute accepts any one of its listed types. `isProofRequired()` decides whether an answer needs proof at all, and `getProofSkipReason()` names why an answer needs none so each reason gets its own badge instead of one blanket "Not needed": `Not applicable` when the answer is not read for the student's year standing (a QPI for an incoming first-year, a senior high school average for an enrolled student), `No proof needed` when the recorded answer claims nothing (IP community or PWD "No", "Not employed", honors "None"), and `Not answered` while an optional answer is still "Not specified". None of them asks for a document. `Not needed` is the single neutral state used before those reasons were separated: it is kept only in `attributeVerificationStatuses`, so a profile snapshot submitted earlier still renders the badge it was frozen with, and `isAttributeProofSettled()` treats it as a state with nothing left to do. A standing-scoped badge follows the standing that decides whether the field is shown, not the saved one: `AcademicSection` in My Profile and the first-login `AcademicProfileModal` re-derive the QPI and senior high school average states against the standing the form is showing, so changing the year standing never labels a mandatory field `Not applicable` before the change is saved. Verifying the document in the Admissions Office review verifies the linked attributes. Verification never blocks eligibility matching or starting an application, but submitting requires a current proof attached for each answer the application relies on (see the readiness rule above). This departs from the manuscript's earlier "informational only" framing and should be reflected in the manuscript.

## Email And SMS Notification Delivery
Notifications stay event-oriented, and delivery runs through Supabase Edge Functions rather than the browser:

- `process-deadline-reminders` is invoked daily at 07:00 Asia/Manila by pg_cron + pg_net (`supabase/migrations/20261005000001_schedule_deadline_reminders.sql`, with the reference block at the bottom of `supabase/schema.sql`). It evaluates the reminder rule on the Manila calendar, writes the in-app row, emails through Resend, texts through iprogSMS, and records the delivery in `notification_email_log`. It runs with `verify_jwt` disabled and authenticates the scheduled caller against `REMINDER_CRON_SECRET` by exact value; a signed-in user's JWT is also accepted for on-demand runs. Never point this endpoint at the anon key.
- `notify-application-status` is fired from the database by an after-update trigger (`supabase/migrations/20261014000003_notify_application_status_from_database.sql`, with the reference block at the bottom of `supabase/schema.sql`) whenever `applications.status` changes. The trigger is the **primary delivery path**, and the app still calls it after a successful status write as a retry for a tab that closed first. Because the two can run for the same change at almost the same moment, the function sends SMS **only for the database caller** (`isTrustedDatabaseCaller`): email dedupes through a Resend idempotency key and the in-app row through its source key, but SMS has neither, so the browser retry covers only the in-app and email channels and preserves whatever the database path recorded in the ledger. Like `process-deadline-reminders`, it runs with `verify_jwt` disabled because the database caller presents the shared `REMINDER_CRON_SECRET` (the platform JWT check rejects service role keys); it authenticates that secret by exact value inside the function, and a signed-in user's JWT is still validated for the browser path. It re-reads the application, recipient, and caller role server-side so the request body cannot spoof the recipient, and it allows only the trusted database caller, an Admissions Office Staff or Head account, the Department Chair for that student's department, or the student who owns the application. For a database-triggered call the message actor is read from `application_status_log.actor_role`. It writes the in-app row with `source_key = application-status-<applicationId>-<status>` and an upsert on `(profile_id, source_key)`, so the trigger and the client retry send at most one in-app row; `inAppEnabled` controls that channel. Email and SMS are handled independently and tracked by `email_status` / `sms_status` in `notification_email_log`, so already-delivered channels are skipped while failed or skipped sends remain retryable.
- `notify-document-verification` tells the student when the Admissions Office verifies or rejects one of their Document Vault files. It follows the same pattern: the database trigger (`20261021000001_notify_document_verification_from_database.sql`) is the **primary delivery path**, and `changeDocumentStatus` in `App.jsx` calls it after the successful write as the idempotent retry. It runs with `verify_jwt` disabled, accepts the exact `REMINDER_CRON_SECRET` for the database caller or a signed-in Admissions Office Staff or Head JWT, re-reads the document, its owner's profile and `notification_preferences`, and the applications that attach the file, and dedupes on `source_key = document-verification-<documentId>-<status>-<epoch(verification_decided_at)>` so a file rejected again after a reset notifies again. A `Rejected` decision goes to in-app (`inAppEnabled`), email (`emailEnabled`, with a Resend idempotency key) and SMS (`smsEnabled`, **database caller only**, preserving the browser retry's ledger like the status function); every message carries the recorded reason and tells the student to replace the file from the application's details. A `Verified` decision is in-app only, so a multi-file application does not send a burst of emails and texts. Deploy it with `supabase functions deploy notify-document-verification --no-verify-jwt`; it uses the same `RESEND_*`, `APP_SITE_URL`, `REMINDER_CRON_SECRET`, and `IPROGSMS_*` secrets.
- `notify-interview-update` announces interview schedule changes (SOP step 5). The database trigger `notify_interview_update_change` (`20261029000001_notify_interview_update_from_database.sql`, placeholders like the other notify migrations) calls it whenever the interview `revision` or `state` changes at `Interview`. The coordinating Admissions Office browser calls it as the retry.
  - Each account panelist gets in-app and email for every new revision or a cancellation (`interview-panel-<applicationId>-<revision>-<state>`).
  - The student gets in-app, email, and SMS (database caller only) for a reschedule or cancellation (`interview-update-<applicationId>-<revision>-<state>`). The first schedule rides `notify-application-status`, whose `Interview` message now carries the date, mode, and location or link.
  - Recording attendance notifies no one.
  - Deploy with `supabase functions deploy notify-interview-update --no-verify-jwt`.
- `manage-interview-event` is the Google Calendar and Meet integration. A signed-in Admissions Office JWT calls it with `status`, `verify`, `create`, `update`, `cancel`, or `sync`.
  - `index.ts` is transport only (CORS, the JWT check, the Staff/Head role gate, JSON). The behavior lives in `_shared/interviewEvent.js` with the Supabase client and `fetch` injected, so `tests/interviewEventService.test.js` covers it without a Deno runtime.
  - It re-reads the attendees from the database (student, account panelists, and faculty guests with an email), hides the guest list from guests so the applicant never sees the panel, and generates a Google Meet link for an online interview without a pasted link. The schedule comes from the request body for `create`/`update` because the app calls Google before it writes the record; `sync` reads the saved record instead.
  - `verify` is a real health check, not a config check: it refreshes the token and calls `events.list` on `GOOGLE_CALENDAR_ID`. Do **not** build it on `calendars.get` or `calendarList` — a `calendar.events`-only token is refused there with 403 `ACCESS_TOKEN_SCOPE_INSUFFICIENT`, which would report a perfectly good setup as broken. `App.jsx` calls `verify` on workspace load and treats `configured` as "a Meet link can be generated right now", so an expired token reads as "re-authorization needed" instead of failing at save.
  - Google messages are mapped to codes in `_shared/googleCalendar.js` (`reauth_required`, `calendar_not_found`, `calendar_forbidden`, `insufficient_scope`, `event_not_found`, `google_unavailable`, `google_unreachable`) and explained by `describeGoogleFailure()`, so the panel never shows a bare `Not Found`. Failures answer 200 with `{ ok: false, code, reason }`.
  - Every event carries `extendedProperties.private` (`scholarpathApplicationId`, `scholarpathRevision`), and the Meet `requestId` is `getMeetRequestId(applicationId, revision)`, so a retried create cannot fork a second event or conference. `sync` finds an event through those properties when the stored id was lost.
  - An online ↔ on-site change replaces the event (delete, then create) instead of sending `conferenceData: null`, which does not reliably strip conferencing. A reschedule reuses the stored meeting link so the applicant never gets two links — but never the link of a **cancelled** interview, because that conference belonged to the event the cancellation removed.
  - **One interview has exactly one live event.** `create` first adopts whatever event the application already owns (the current ledger row, then any event carrying its `scholarpathApplicationId` extended property) instead of adding a second. `cancel` deletes every event attributable to the application (request body, record, ledger, extended properties) and runs before the record forgets the id, so a failed delete is reported instead of orphaning a live invitation. `sync` sweeps duplicates and attaches a Meet conference to an online event that has none. `cancelInterview` in `App.jsx` stores `calendar.removedAt` only after Google confirms the delete; a null `removedAt` on a cancelled interview is what the tab's *Calendar event still live* badge and its **Repair calendar event** action read.
  - `interview_calendar_events` is the provider-side **history**, not one row per application (`20261030000000_add_interview_calendar_events.sql`, reshaped by `20261031000000_interview_calendar_event_history.sql`): `ledger_id` is the key, every provider event keeps its own row, and `is_current` marks the one `applications.interview.calendar` points at, with a partial unique index enforcing one current row per application. A superseded or failed row stays so a repair can find and remove an event the record no longer names. RLS is enabled with no policies; only the service role writes it, and `sync` repairs from it.
  - The `.ics` fallback (`buildInterviewIcs`) uses a stable `UID` of `<applicationId>@scholarpath-addu` with `SEQUENCE` = revision, `METHOD:REQUEST` (or `CANCEL`), and exactly one `ATTENDEE` per file, so a reschedule updates the entry and a shared file can never expose the panel.
  - Secrets: `GOOGLE_OAUTH_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_OAUTH_CLIENT_SECRET`, `GOOGLE_CALENDAR_REFRESH_TOKEN` (one office Google account consents once to the `calendar.events` scope; mint it with `npm run google:auth`, which is `scripts/google-calendar-auth.mjs`), and `GOOGLE_CALENDAR_ID`. Copy the calendar id with a literal `@`; the default `primary` puts interviews on the organizer's personal calendar. Never a `VITE_` prefix.
  - The consenting account must own the interview calendar, or hold "Make changes to events" on it. A valid token for a different account answers 404 for that calendar — the most common setup failure.
  - Without the secrets it answers `{ configured: false }` and the schedule form requires a pasted https meeting link.
  - While the OAuth consent screen is External + Testing, refresh tokens expire after 7 days: re-run `npm run google:auth` and update the secret. Publishing the app or consenting with an internal Workspace app removes the limit.
  - Deploy with `supabase functions deploy manage-interview-event --no-verify-jwt`.
- `send-test-email` backs Settings -> Email delivery test and only ever sends to the signed-in caller; `send-test-sms` is its SMS counterpart and text messages only the caller's own `profiles.phone` number (echoed back masked).
- Shared, dependency-free modules live in `supabase/functions/_shared/`: `email.js` (Resend-compatible table HTML, plain-text alternatives, and the delivery-preference checks), `reminders.js` (the reminder math shared with `src/App.jsx`), `resend.js` (the minimal email fetch client), and `sms.js` (iprogSMS client, Philippine mobile normalization, and SMS text renderers). They are plain JavaScript so Deno, the vitest suite, and the client bundle can all import them (for example, `validateMobileNumber` in `src/lib/profile.js` reuses `formatPhilippineMobile` for the onboarding modal and My Profile, and Settings reuses `maskPhilippineMobile`).
- Delivery is opt-in per channel and per reminder. `profiles.notification_preferences` is the only server-side input: the in-app deadline row needs `inAppEnabled` plus the matching `deadlineReminders` offset, the email needs `emailEnabled` plus the same offset, and the SMS needs `smsEnabled` plus the same offset and a normalizable `profiles.phone` value. Status notifications honor `inAppEnabled`, `emailEnabled`, and `smsEnabled`; in-app rows dedupe on the unique `notifications(profile_id, source_key)` key, while email and SMS deliveries are independently deduped/retried through their channel statuses in `notification_email_log`. `isInAppEnabledForUser()` in `_shared/email.js` defaults in-app status alerts to enabled unless explicitly disabled.
- `notifications.channel` is constrained to `SMS` / `Email` / `In-app`, so carry the deadline kind in the notification title and body instead of adding a channel value.
- Email requires the verified Resend sending domain and the Edge Function secrets (`RESEND_API_KEY`, `RESEND_FROM_EMAIL`, `RESEND_FROM_NAME`, `APP_SITE_URL`, `REMINDER_CRON_SECRET`); SMS requires `IPROGSMS_API_TOKEN` (with optional `IPROGSMS_PROVIDER`, the gateway's `sms_provider` 0/1/2 flag). They are server-side only and must never carry a `VITE_` prefix. A send only happens when the Supabase workspace is loaded, and either gateway may be left unset without breaking the app (sends are skipped, never thrown).
- The email CTA links use `?view=calendar`, `?view=applications`, and `?view=settings`, but the app does not read a `view` query parameter yet, so a recipient lands on the role's landing view. Do not describe those links as deep links until `App.jsx` handles the parameter.
- iprogSMS constraints: a 200 response confirms queue-accept (`message_id`) only — there are no delivery webhooks, so ledger rows describe queue-accept rather than confirmed carrier delivery (poll-only status via `GET /sms_messages/status`). Globe, TM, DITO, and GOMO recipients use the shared `iprogSMS` sender; Smart and TNT require a purchased custom sender name.

Keep the local and server shapes identical so the two never disagree.

Existing Supabase projects receive it from `20261010000000_add_application_notification_source_key.sql`, and `supabase/schema.sql` must stay aligned.

The same migration removes older duplicate pairs before adding the index. The helper is `async` and returns a real Promise: a Supabase Postgrest builder is only a thenable (`.then` with no `.catch`/`.finally`), so a fire-and-forget caller must wrap it (`Promise.resolve(...)` before `.catch(...)`) or await it. The endorsement itself is recorded on `applications.endorsement` by the Admissions Office Head, so nothing writes a new row here; the table keeps the historical Chair decisions readable.
- `staffDirectory`, `interviewLocations`, `interviewPanelists`, and `interviewNotes` are the SOP step 5 session slices. `loadSupabaseWorkspace()` loads them for staff roles only: the directory comes from the profiles select, which now includes `role`; Department Chairs get no locations; only active panelists are loaded. A student session keeps them empty, and sign-out clears them with the rest of the session state.
- `reviewComments` is the staff-only endorsement thread loaded from `application_review_comments` (`20261023000000_add_application_review_comments.sql`) by `loadSupabaseWorkspace()` for staff roles only, appended locally through `createSupabaseReviewComment()` after a successful insert, and cleared on sign-out with the rest of the session state. The rows are append-only, have no student policy, and a Department Chair may only read/write a case in their own department.
- Application status writes persist the timeline with the status and any stage payload: `submitSupabaseApplication(id, { timeline, statement, profileSnapshot, certifiedAt })`, `updateSupabaseApplicationStatus(id, status, timeline, stagePatch)`, and `updateSupabaseApplicationStage()` return `{ error }` so the caller notifies only after a successful write. `changeApplicationStatus` awaits `updateSupabaseApplicationStatus()`, keeps the local row unchanged on failure, and returns `{ ok }` / `{ error }` to the review surface. Submission and status events should remain part of the application record, not synthetic client-only notifications.

The database RLS policy is in `20261010000001_limit_chair_transitions_and_manage_internal_scholarships.sql`.

- The workspace also returns a compact `students` directory for visible profiles. Admissions Office document verification uses it to compare linked QPI and household-income proof against the student's declared profile values; owner labels fall back to the document's loaded owner fields.
- Application rejection requires a recorded reason through `ReasonModal`; it is saved to the application's timeline.
- Document Vault rejection also requires a recorded reason: the Reject action in `ApplicationReviewModal` opens `ReasonModal` with the preset `documentRejectionReasons` dropdown (`src/lib/constants.js`) and optional notes (required for "Other"; stored as "<reason> — <notes>"), and `changeDocumentStatus` in `App.jsx` saves it through `updateSupabaseDocumentStatus(id, status, reason)` to `documents.rejection_reason` (`20261020000000_add_document_rejection_reason.sql`). The `require_document_rejection_reason` trigger refuses a move into `Rejected` without a reason and clears the reason when the file leaves `Rejected`; `protect_document_review_fields` keeps the column staff-owned. `DocumentRejectionReason` (`ApplicationParts.jsx`) shows the reason in the review pane, the Document Vault card, and the application workspace. Only the Admissions Office moves an application to `For Verification`, which records the action in the timeline and sends the normal status notification after a successful status write.
- Application-status in-app notifications are written by `notify-application-status` after the Supabase status write succeeds. Do not add synthetic client-side application-status notifications; staff changes belong in application timelines, and student alerts use the server's stable source key.
- `validateFinancialSection()` does not require `hasActiveGovernmentGrant`; that account/profile field remains supported, but it is not a required financial-section answer.
- Profile data has three local homes. `authUser` holds the account fields (`fullName`, `phone`, `bio`, `studentNumber`, `degreeProgram`, `department`, `qpi`, `householdIncome`, `hasActiveGovernmentGrant`), mirrored to the core `profiles` columns. `profileDraft` holds what the Smart Eligibility Checker reads, including the extended attributes listed in `ELIGIBILITY_ATTRIBUTE_KEYS` (`src/lib/profile.js`) — which now also carry the ranked `programChoice2` / `programChoice3` (an incoming first-year's 2nd and 3rd program choices) and the self-reported `ipCommunity`, `pwd`, and `employed` answers — plus the descriptive fields listed in `PROFILE_DETAIL_KEYS` (religion, civil status, address, country, family details, and the default scholarship statement `essay`). The eligibility attributes are mirrored to `profiles.eligibility_attributes`; the descriptive fields are mirrored to `profiles.profile_details`. Save through `saveProfile` / `updateProfileFields`, which write only the keys present on the patch, and keep the key lists in `src/lib/profile.js` as the single source.
- `profiles.bio` and `profiles.eligibility_attributes` come from `supabase/migrations/20261005140000_add_profile_details.sql`; `profiles.profile_details` comes from `supabase/migrations/20261008000000_add_profile_details_json.sql`. `getProfileDetails()` reads them in separate queries and `updateProfileFields()` writes each group in its own statement, reporting `detailsSynced: false` when a column is missing, so a project with a migration pending still signs in and saves the rest. Do not add these columns to the `getUserProfile()` select: a missing column would fail the whole query and reopen the onboarding modal.
- Citizenship, IP community, PWD, and employment status are self-reported and verifiable through the Document Vault (`VERIFIABLE_ATTRIBUTE_OPTIONS`), like QPI and household income. Verification never blocks eligibility matching; a claimed answer (IP community or PWD "Yes", employed) needs a current proof attached before an application is submitted, and a "No" answer reads `No proof needed`. Class size remains part of the Eligibility background section and the Jubilee honors criterion.
- Saving a QPI through `updateUserProfile` or `updateProfileFields` also upserts the current academic year in `annual_qpi_records`.
- New localStorage state must be merged with defaults so older saved sessions remain loadable; do not assume `customDeadlines` or notification preferences exist in older records.
